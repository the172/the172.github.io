About

Reporting from Washington on the forces reshaping Korea, the United States, and the world.

Wonchul Kim is Chief of the Washington Bureau at Hankyoreh Media. He has worked as a reporter and editor covering U.S. politics, foreign policy, Korea–U.S. relations, national security, trade, criminal justice, and Korean politics.

His recent reporting ranges from the Korean Peninsula and U.S.–China competition to defense and shipbuilding, tariffs and industrial policy, Iran and the Middle East, and the accelerating development of advanced artificial intelligence.
